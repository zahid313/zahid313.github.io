## 💳 3. Google Play Developer Registration & Payment Insights

* **One-Time Fee:** $25 USD.
* **Card Requirements:** Must be an international **Visa** or **Mastercard** debit/credit card (no prepaid/gift cards).
* **Crucial Rule:** The **Account Country**, **Payments Profile Country**, and **Card Issuing Country** must match 1-to-1 to avoid payment rejections (`[OR-CCSEH-05]`) or identity verification holds.
* **Option A (Japan):**
  - Register as Japan using Japanese debit card.
  - Billed ~$27.50 with 10% JCT.
  - Submit W-8BEN form with Japanese *My Number* to reduce US withholding tax from 30% to 0%.
  - Verify with Japanese Residence Card (*在留カード*).
* **Option B (Pakistan):**
  - **Method 1:** Account in brother's name in Pakistan with his card & CNIC, then invite your Google account as **Admin**.
  - **Method 2:** Register in your name using your own Pakistani **SadaPay** (Virtual Mastercard) or **NayaPay** (Virtual Visa) debit card funded with PKR ~8,000.
