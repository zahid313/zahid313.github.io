This Privacy Policy describes how Apex Roofing & Storm Estimator ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our") handles your information. We value your privacy and are committed to maintaining the highest level of security and transparency.

1. Offline-First Architecture & Data Storage

Apex Roofing & Storm Estimator operates on an offline-first architecture. All core features, calculations, tools, and user records are processed directly on your local device.

🔒 Local Device Persistence

Roof measurements, pitch ratios, material quantities, and quote archives are saved exclusively on your device.

This data is stored inside your device's sandboxed local storage (via React Native AsyncStorage / SQLite). We do not operate backend servers that collect, store, transmit, or sell your personal data.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Application is supported by advertising provided by Google AdMob and rotating sponsored placements. To serve contextually relevant advertisements, prevent ad fraud, and calculate metrics, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically process certain pseudonymous device and network data, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID) / Identifier for Advertisers
  • IP address (for coarse geographic location and fraud prevention)
  • Device model, manufacturer, operating system version, and screen resolution
  • Ad interaction metrics (views, clicks, and completion rates)

We do not combine this advertising telemetry with any personal records or identities. You can review Google's privacy policies and opt out of interest-based ads via the links below:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • Google AdMob Data & Privacy Guidelines
  • Google Ad Personalization Controls

3. In-App Purchases & "Remove Ads" (Pro)

The Application includes an optional "Remove Ads" feature that grants an ad-free experience. When purchasing upgrades:

  • All financial transactions, payments, and receipt validations are handled entirely by Google Play Billing.
  • We never see, receive, or store your credit card, bank details, or billing address.
  • Your ad-free status is permanently verified and stored in your device's secure local storage.

4. Application Permissions

The Application requests only the minimal permissions strictly necessary for its operation:

  • INTERNET (for AdMob advertising)
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / READ_MEDIA (for exporting PDF bids)

You can adjust or revoke permissions at any time through your Android device settings (Settings > Apps > Apex Roofing & Storm Estimator > Permissions).

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our application is not targeted toward individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect, store, or solicit personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe that a child under 13 has provided information without parental consent, please contact us so we can take immediate corrective measures.

6. European Privacy Rights (GDPR) & California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to Know & Access: Because all user data resides locally on your device, you have full and direct access to your data at all times.
  • Right to Erasure / Deletion: You can completely delete all locally stored data at any time by clearing the app data in your device settings or uninstalling the application.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Personalized Ads: You can reset or opt out of personalized advertising IDs via your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Delete Advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization).
  • We Do Not Sell Personal Information: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.

7. Data Security

Your local data is safeguarded by Android's application sandboxing, which prevents other applications from accessing your local files and databases. When communicating with Google AdMob for ad delivery, all network requests are encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
