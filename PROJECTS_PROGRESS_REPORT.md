## 🎯 Production Google Play Store Deployment Checklist

When you are ready to publish these applications publicly to the Google Play Console:

1. **AdMob Live Units**:
   - Open [admob.google.com](https://admob.google.com), create your app properties, and copy your live **App ID** and **Ad Unit IDs**.
   - Update `appId` in `AndroidManifest.xml` and `src/config/ads.ts` for each app.
2. **Release Keystore**:
   - Generate a private release keystore via `keytool`:
     ```bash
     keytool -genkey -v -keystore release.keystore -alias release-key -keyalg RSA -keysize 2048 -validity 10000
     ```
3. **Generate Google Play App Bundles (`.aab`)**:
   - Run `gradlew.bat bundleRelease` in each app's `android/` directory.
   - Upload the resulting `.aab` file from `android/app/build/outputs/bundle/release/` to the Google Play Console.
